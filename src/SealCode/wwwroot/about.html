Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

SealCode is a shared code editor. You can join a room using its link. Administrators can also sign in with a Google account from an organization allowed by the site operator. This policy explains how SealCode uses data for that sign-in and for shared rooms.

Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, SealCode receives a signed identity token from Google. We verify the token and use your Google account identifier, verified email address, and organization domain to decide whether you may access room management. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or Google password.

We use your account identifier to recognize you as the creator of rooms. Your email address is displayed as the creator of those rooms to other signed-in administrators.

Storage and retention

We use the Google identity token only to complete the sign-in request. After sign-in, your browser receives an essential, secure admin session cookie containing your account identifier, email address, and organization domain. The cookie expires after eight hours; signing out clears it sooner. SealCode does not keep a separate database of Google accounts.

Room documents, room ownership, and participant names are held in server memory while rooms exist. They are removed when a room is deleted or the server restarts. Operational logs may contain room identifiers, room names, participant names, and the account identifier of a room creator; log retention depends on the server's hosting configuration.

The hosting service may also process technical request data, such as IP addresses and request times, to deliver and protect the site.

Sharing

We do not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. Other signed-in administrators can see your email address on rooms you create. Google processes the sign-in flow under its own privacy policy.
